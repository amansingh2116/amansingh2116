### 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

Data Science & Quantitative Researcher associated with the **Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata**. Focused on building mathematical and computational solutions at the intersection of **Quantitative Finance, Empirical Asset Pricing, Statistical Machine Learning**, and **Production Full-Stack Financial Systems**.

- 🎯 **Quantitative Finance**: Portfolio intelligence, mutual fund multi-factor scoring, systematic IPO research, and global equity valuation.
- 🔬 **Statistical ML & Vision**: Custom OCR architecture design, statistical factor models (PCA), background matte extraction algorithms.
- 🚀 **Full-Stack Engineering**: Architecting and deploying financial research platforms with Django, Pandas, Plotly, and LaTeX automated report engines.
